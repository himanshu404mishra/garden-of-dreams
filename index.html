Kaiser Shumsher Rana

The story

One man’s vision, quietly restored.

Field Marshal Kaiser Shumsher Jung Bahadur Rana was a statesman, scholar and lover of gardens, art and literature. He built six pavilions here, one for each of Nepal’s seasons. Neglected for decades, the garden has been restored and is open to all.
