Kaiser Cafe

Kaiser Cafe, by Basera Boutique Hotel.

Open to the public inside the garden. Please visit and dine in style.

Kaiser Cafe serves international standard cuisine, with Nepalese and Continental food for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Admission to the garden is required to reach the cafe. It is the main source of income that sustains the garden.
