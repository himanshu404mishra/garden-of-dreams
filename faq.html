FAQ

Good questions.

Quick answers for first-time visitors.

What time does the garden open?

Every day of the week, from 9:00am to 9:00pm.

Can I bring my own food?

No. Kaiser Cafe, managed by Basera Boutique Hotel, offers food and drinks inside the garden.

Do I need a ticket to visit Kaiser Cafe?

Yes. You buy a garden ticket first and then walk to the cafe. Admission fees are the main source of income that sustains the garden.

Is there a restaurant inside?

Yes. Kaiser Cafe serves international standard cuisine, with Nepalese and Continental food for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Can we play games or play loud music?

No. The garden is neither a party palace nor a playground, and loud sound disturbs other visitors.

Who manages the garden?

The Garden of Dreams Development Board, an autonomous body under Nepal's Ministry of Education.
