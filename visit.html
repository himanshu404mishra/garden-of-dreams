Plan your visit

Open daily, 9:00am to 9:00pm.

Tickets are sold at the entrance. Guards may ask to see yours before you enter the garden.

Discounts

  • 50% off every day for children, senior citizens and differently-abled visitors.
  • 25% off when you buy ten tickets.

Good to know

  • No outside food. Kaiser Cafe is inside the garden.
  • No games or sports. It is neither a party palace nor a playground.
  • No loud music or sound, out of respect for other visitors.
  • Cafe visitors need a garden ticket first.

Getting here

Garden of Dreams, Tridevi Sadak, Kathmandu 44600, just across the street from the former Royal Palace at the entrance to Thamel.
